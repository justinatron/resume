Professional Summary

IT professional with hands-on experience in network infrastructure, end-user support, and live event technology operations. Skilled in network switch configuration, VLAN management, copper and fiber patching, troubleshooting, and providing technical support in fast-paced environments. I am passionate about technology and especially interested in networking, infrastructure, and the ways technology can be used to create better experiences for the people who rely on it.

My interest in technology is rooted in a genuine desire to help people. Coming from a background in retail and public health, I have always been drawn to environments where I can interact with people, understand their needs, solve problems, and make their experiences better. These experiences taught me that good support is not only about having the technical knowledge to solve a problem, but also about listening, communicating clearly, remaining patient, and making people feel comfortable asking for help.

My interest in technology and gaming started at a young age. I remember my parents bringing home our first desktop computer and spending hours exploring the ins and outs of it. I was always the one peeking in the system settings, building slideshows for fun,spending hours in Purple Palace, and helping my family troubleshoot computer issues. That curiosity grew into a genuine understanding of how technology supports people when it is structured, reliable, and well understood, and empowers people; I desire to help others feel the same. Through my continuous rants about cool features I found and how-to tutorials, my biggest accomplishment was helping my parents grasp the concept of not only how technology works, but best practices to stay safe as well. Those early experiences helped me recognize that I enjoyed figuring out how things worked and, even more importantly, helping others understand and use technology.
